# Product Excellence Audit — 2026-07-21

Audited base: `a02aa04` (origin/main, PR #535 merge). Branch: `feat/product-excellence-batch`.

Method: six sequential investigation panels (data truth, student journey, learning science, UX/accessibility, reliability/performance, adversarial synthesis) — 30 specialized read-only investigators plus 5 fresh adversarial reviewers. Every finding below was independently re-verified against current code by a second agent (and the highest-impact ones by the lead session directly); learning-science recommendations cite official GMAC pages or peer-reviewed literature. Verdict totals: **138 confirmed, 32 duplicates merged, 6 downgraded, 3 rejected**. Scores are `(impact 1-5 × frequency 1-5 × confidence 0-1) / effort 1-5`.

Everything below respects the mission exclusions (no marketing, consent/tracking, payments/auth, migrations, question content, PR #535 selection policy, First 48 Hours, official-exam roadmap internals, production data). Findings that *require* an excluded area are recorded in §5 and not implemented.
