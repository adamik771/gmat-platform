## Q1
**difficulty:** Easy
**type:** Problem Solving
**topic:** Fractions

What is the value of 3/4 + 5/8 - 1/2?

- A) 3/8
- B) 5/8
- C) 7/8
- D) 1
- E) 9/8

**answer:** C
**explanation:** To add and subtract fractions, we first express each fraction in terms of a common denominator, after which we operate on the numerators alone.

Let S = 3/4 + 5/8 - 1/2. The denominators are 4, 8, and 2, whose least common denominator is 8.

Converting each fraction to eighths, we have 3/4 = 6/8, 5/8 = 5/8, and 1/2 = 4/8.

Substituting these equivalent fractions gives S = 6/8 + 5/8 - 4/8.

Because the denominators are now identical, we combine the numerators: 6 + 5 - 4 = 7. Thus S = 7/8.

The correct answer is C.

## Q2

**type:** Problem Solving
**topic:** Percentages

A shirt originally priced at $80 is discounted by 15%. What is the sale price of the shirt?

- A) $12
- B) $65
- C) $68
- D) $72
- E) $76

**answer:** C
**explanation:** When a price is reduced by a given percent, the resulting sale price is the remaining percent of the original price. A discount of 15% means that 15% of the price is removed, so the customer pays the remaining 85% of the original price. We may therefore express the sale price as a single multiplication: the original price times the decimal equivalent of the remaining percent.

Let p denote the original price, so p = 80. The remaining percent after a 15% discount is 100% - 15% = 85%, whose decimal equivalent is 0.85. The sale price s is then

s = 0.85 * p = 0.85 * 80 = 68.

Equivalently, we may first compute the dollar amount of the discount and subtract it from the original price. The discount equals 0.15 * 80 = 12, and the sale price is 80 - 12 = 68. Both methods yield the same result.

The correct answer is C.

**answer:** C

Let the red marbles equal 3 parts and the blue marbles equal 5 parts, in accordance with the given ratio of 3 to 5. The total number of parts is therefore 3 + 5 = 8.

These 8 parts together account for all 40 marbles, so the value of one part is 40 / 8 = 5 marbles.

Since the red marbles correspond to 3 parts, the number of red marbles is 3 * 5 = 15.

As a check, the blue marbles correspond to 5 parts, giving 5 * 5 = 25, and 15 + 25 = 40, which matches the stated total.

The correct answer is C.

## Q5
**difficulty:** Medium
**type:** Problem Solving
**topic:** Percentages

A store raises the price of an item by 20% and then offers a 20% discount on the new price. The final price is what percent of the original price?

- A) 92%
- B) 96%
- C) 100%
- D) 104%
- E) 108%

**answer:** B
**explanation:** Successive percent changes are combined by multiplying their scale factors rather than by adding or subtracting the percentages. An increase of p percent corresponds to multiplication by (1 + p/100), and a decrease of p percent corresponds to multiplication by (1 - p/100). To compare the final price with the original, we apply these factors in sequence and read off the result.

Let P represent the original price of the item. The store first raises the price by 20 percent. A 20 percent increase multiplies the price by 1 + 20/100 = 1.20, so the new price is 1.20P.

The store then offers a 20 percent discount on this new price. A 20 percent decrease multiplies the price by 1 - 20/100 = 0.80. Applying this factor to the new price gives a final price of

0.80 (1.20P) = (0.80)(1.20) P = 0.96P.

Thus the final price is 0.96P, which is 96 percent of the original price P.

The correct answer is B.

If 2^x = 32 and 3^y = 81, what is the value of x + y?

- A) 7
- B) 8

- D) 10
- E) 12

**answer:** C
**explanation:** This problem applies the principle that if two powers with the same base are equal, then their exponents must be equal. Each given equation can be solved by re-expressing the constant on the right-hand side as a power of the same base that appears on the left.

Consider first the equation 2^x = 32. We express 32 as a power of 2. Since 2^5 = 32, the equation becomes 2^x = 2^5. Equating the exponents gives x = 5.

Consider next the equation 3^y = 81. We express 81 as a power of 3. Since 3^4 = 81, the equation becomes 3^y = 3^4. Equating the exponents gives y = 4.

Finally, we compute the requested sum:

x + y = 5 + 4 = 9.

The correct answer is C.

Let the common part size be x grams. The recipe requires flour, sugar, and butter in the ratio 5:3:2, so flour corresponds to 5x grams, sugar to 3x grams, and butter to 2x grams.

We are told that the baker uses 750 grams of flour. Because flour represents 5 parts, we have

5x = 750.

Dividing both sides by 5 gives

x = 750 / 5 = 150.

Thus each part equals 150 grams. Butter corresponds to 2 parts, so the amount of butter required is

2x = 2 * 150 = 300 grams.

The correct answer is C.

Let x be the unknown. The given condition is |2x - 7| = 11. Since 11 is positive, the expression inside the absolute-value bars must equal either 11 or -11.

Consider the first case, 2x - 7 = 11. Adding 7 to both sides gives 2x = 18, and dividing both sides by 2 gives x = 9.

Consider the second case, 2x - 7 = -11. Adding 7 to both sides gives 2x = -4, and dividing both sides by 2 gives x = -2.

The two possible values of x are therefore 9 and -2. Their sum is 9 + (-2) = 7.

Dividing the numerator by the denominator gives 72 / 6 = 12.

## Q13

**type:** Problem Solving
**topic:** Fractions

Which of the following is the simplified form of 42/98?

- A) 2/7
- B) 3/7
- C) 4/7
- D) 6/14
- E) 21/49

**answer:** B
**explanation:** A fraction is in simplified form, or lowest terms, when its numerator and denominator share no common factor greater than 1. To reduce a fraction to lowest terms, we divide both the numerator and the denominator by their greatest common factor (GCF).

Let the fraction be 42/98. We first express the numerator and the denominator as products of prime factors:

42 = 2 × 3 × 7
98 = 2 × 7 × 7

The prime factors common to both numbers are 2 and 7, so the greatest common factor is

GCF = 2 × 7 = 14.

Dividing the numerator and the denominator by 14 gives

42 ÷ 14 = 3
98 ÷ 14 = 7,

so 42/98 = 3/7.

Since 3 and 7 share no common factor greater than 1, the fraction 3/7 is fully simplified.

The correct answer is B.

## Q18
**difficulty:** Medium
**type:** Data Sufficiency
**topic:** Fractions

Is the fraction p/q greater than 1/2?

(1) p + q = 20
(2) q = 2p - 5

- A) Statement (1) ALONE is sufficient, but statement (2) alone is not sufficient.
- B) Statement (2) ALONE is sufficient, but statement (1) alone is not sufficient.
- C) BOTH statements TOGETHER are sufficient, but NEITHER statement ALONE is sufficient.
- D) EACH statement ALONE is sufficient.
- E) Statements (1) and (2) TOGETHER are NOT sufficient.

**answer:** C
**explanation:** The question asks whether p/q > 1/2. The governing principle is that a fraction inequality can be analyzed by cross-multiplication, but the direction of the resulting inequality depends on the sign of the denominator: when q > 0, the relation p/q > 1/2 is equivalent to 2p > q, whereas when q < 0 the inequality reverses. A definitive answer therefore requires knowing the values, or at least the signs, of both p and q.

Consider statement (1) alone, which gives p + q = 20. Let p = 5 and q = 15; then p/q = 5/15 = 1/3, which is less than 1/2, so the answer is No. Now let p = 15 and q = 5; then p/q = 15/5 = 3, which is greater than 1/2, so the answer is Yes. Two permissible cases produce opposite answers, so statement (1) alone is not sufficient.

Consider statement (2) alone, which gives q = 2p - 5. Let p = 1; then q = 2(1) - 5 = -3, so p/q = 1/(-3) = -1/3, which is less than 1/2, giving the answer No. Now let p = 4; then q = 2(4) - 5 = 3, so p/q = 4/3, which is greater than 1/2, giving the answer Yes. Two permissible cases again produce opposite answers, so statement (2) alone is not sufficient.

Now combine the two statements. Substitute q = 2p - 5 from statement (2) into p + q = 20 from statement (1):

p + (2p - 5) = 20
3p - 5 = 20
3p = 25
p = 25/3.

Then q = 2p - 5 = 2(25/3) - 5 = 50/3 - 15/3 = 35/3. Both p and q are uniquely determined and positive, so p/q = (25/3)/(35/3) = 25/35 = 5/7. Since 5/7 is greater than 1/2, the answer is a definitive Yes. With both statements together the values of p and q are fixed, so the two statements together are sufficient, though neither alone is sufficient.

The correct answer is C.

## Q20
**difficulty:** Hard
**type:** Data Sufficiency
**topic:** Arithmetic Sequences

The sum of the first n terms of a certain sequence equals 3n² + 2n. What is the value of the 10th term?

(1) The first term of the sequence is 5.
(2) The common difference between consecutive terms is 6.

- A) Statement (1) ALONE is sufficient, but statement (2) alone is not sufficient.
- B) Statement (2) ALONE is sufficient, but statement (1) alone is not sufficient.
- C) BOTH statements TOGETHER are sufficient, but NEITHER statement ALONE is sufficient.
- D) EACH statement ALONE is sufficient.
- E) Statements (1) and (2) TOGETHER are NOT sufficient.

**answer:** D
**explanation:** In any sequence, the nth term equals the difference between the sum of the first n terms and the sum of the first n-1 terms; that is, a_n = S_n - S_(n-1). Because the question provides a formula for S_n that holds for every value of n, the entire sequence is determined before either statement is consulted, and the first task is therefore to test whether the information in the stem alone answers the question.

Let S_n = 3n^2 + 2n denote the sum of the first n terms. Then the sum of the first n-1 terms is S_(n-1) = 3(n-1)^2 + 2(n-1). Expanding gives 3(n^2 - 2n + 1) + 2n - 2 = 3n^2 - 6n + 3 + 2n - 2 = 3n^2 - 4n + 1.

Subtracting yields the nth term: a_n = S_n - S_(n-1) = (3n^2 + 2n) - (3n^2 - 4n + 1) = 6n - 1. Substituting n = 10 gives a_10 = 6(10) - 1 = 59. The stem alone produces a single numerical value for the 10th term, so the question is answerable without any additional information.

Because the stem by itself is sufficient, each statement, considered together with the stem, also yields the same unique value. We may confirm that the two statements are consistent with the sequence already determined: the first term is a_1 = 6(1) - 1 = 5, matching statement (1), and the common difference is a_(n+1) - a_n = (6(n+1) - 1) - (6n - 1) = 6, matching statement (2). Each statement therefore merely restates information already implied by the stem, and each alone is sufficient to determine the 10th term.

The correct answer is D.

## Q21
**difficulty:** Medium
**type:** Problem Solving
**topic:** Mixtures — Acid Solution

A chemist has 15 kilograms of an alloy that is 40% zinc by weight; the rest is copper. How many kilograms of a second alloy that is 70% zinc must be added to produce a new mixture that is 55% zinc by weight?

- A) 10
- B) 12
- C) 15
- D) 18
- E) 20

**answer:** C
**explanation:** This is a mixture problem, which is solved by balancing the mass of the active ingredient (here, zinc) rather than by averaging the percentage concentrations. The principle is that the total weight of zinc contained in the two original alloys must equal the total weight of zinc in the final mixture.

Let x = the number of kilograms of the second alloy (70% zinc) that must be added.

The first alloy weighs 15 kilograms and is 40% zinc, so it contributes 0.40(15) = 6 kilograms of zinc. The second alloy weighs x kilograms and is 70% zinc, so it contributes 0.70x kilograms of zinc. The resulting mixture weighs 15 + x kilograms and must be 55% zinc, so it contains 0.55(15 + x) kilograms of zinc.

Setting the zinc contributed by the two alloys equal to the zinc in the final mixture gives the equation:

0.40(15) + 0.70x = 0.55(15 + x)

We now expand each side:

6 + 0.70x = 8.25 + 0.55x

We collect the x terms on one side and the constants on the other:

0.70x - 0.55x = 8.25 - 6

0.15x = 2.25

We divide both sides by 0.15:

x = 2.25 / 0.15 = 15

Therefore, 15 kilograms of the second alloy must be added.

The correct answer is C.

- 8/11 is short by 3/11
- 11/14 is short by 3/14

For two positive integers, the greatest common factor is 12 and the least common multiple is 180. If one of the integers is 36, what is the other integer?

- B) 60
- C) 72
- D) 90
- E) 144

**answer:** B

What is the units digit of the sum 3^1 + 3^2 + 3^3 + ... + 3^50?

Within each complete cycle of four consecutive terms the units digits sum to 3 + 9 + 7 + 1 = 20, contributing a units digit of 0. ## Q77
**difficulty:** Hard
**type:** Problem Solving
**topic:** Divisibility

What is the largest integer that must be a divisor of n^5 − n for every positive integer n?

- A) 6
- B) 10
- C) 15
- D) 30
- E) 60

**answer:** D
**explanation:** We factor the expression: n^5 − n = n(n^4 − 1) = n(n^2 − 1)(n^2 + 1) = (n − 1)n(n + 1)(n^2 + 1). The factor (n − 1)n(n + 1) is a product of three consecutive integers, so it is divisible by both 2 and 3, hence by 6. For divisibility by 5, we use Fermat's little theorem (or direct checking of residues): n^5 ≡ n (mod 5) for every integer n, so 5 always divides n^5 − n. Since 2, 3, and 5 are distinct primes each guaranteed to divide the expression, their product 30 must divide it for all n. Testing n = 2 gives 32 − 2 = 30, confirming that no larger fixed divisor is possible (60 fails here). The correct answer is D.
