# Escalation Rules

When and how to hand something up to Adam. The principle is simple: **escalate rarely, but always for
the red list.** Adam's time is reserved for serious leads and real risks — everything routine, you
handle yourself with the approved scripts.

This is one of the "Adam does only 5 things" commitments: one of his five things is to **answer serious
leads only.** Escalation is how a serious lead reaches him. Do not escalate routine work, and do not
let a true serious lead sit unescalated.

Sibling docs:
- `LEAD_REPLY_DECISION_TREE.md` — the RED branches that trigger escalation (cross-referenced).
- `OUTREACH_QA_RULES.md` — the can-say / cannot-say rules; "when unsure, escalate" lives there too.
- `OUTREACH_SCRIPTS.md` — the approved scripts; off-script means escalate.
- `MESSAGE_APPROVAL_WORKFLOW.md` — new template types go to "Pending approval," not escalation.

---

## 1. Escalation triggers (escalate if ANY of these is true)

- **Serious lead** — wants a call, is ready to pay, is negotiating, or needs a custom answer
  (custom pricing, a tailored plan, a partnership). See the definition in
  `LEAD_REPLY_DECISION_TREE.md`.
- **Pricing or discount beyond the approved codes** — any custom number, a new code, a bulk/seat
  deal, or a price the person is pushing back on. The only codes you may reference are FOUNDING30 and
  FOUNDING40 (`OUTREACH_QA_RULES.md`). Anything beyond them escalates.
- **Complaint, upset person, public criticism, or any legal / PR risk.**
- **Press or media** — any journalist, blogger, podcaster, or media request.
- **Anything off-script** — a request the approved scripts and snippets do not cover.
- **Technical or site issues** — bugs, signup/login problems, checkout errors, broken links,
  anything about how the platform itself is behaving.
- **Any ethical doubt** — anything that feels like it might cross a line in `OUTREACH_QA_RULES.md`,
  or where you are simply unsure. When unsure, escalate.

If none of these is true and the reply is GREEN or YELLOW, do **not** escalate — handle it yourself.

---

## 2. How to escalate — the lead card

Escalate by posting a concise **lead card** to the agreed channel (the CRM "Escalations" view or the
shared escalation doc — whatever Adam uses). Keep it short and complete so Adam can decide in under a
minute. Use this exact format:

```
LEAD CARD
- Name:            [full name]
- Link / profile:  [LinkedIn URL, email, or where the conversation is]
- Context:         [how they came in — segment, channel, which template, prior touches]
- What they want:  [the request in one or two sentences]
- Why escalating:  [which trigger from Section 1]
- Suggested next:  [your recommended next step, if you have one]
```

Example:

```
LEAD CARD
- Name:            Mariam K.
- Link / profile:  linkedin.com/in/example  (DM thread)
- Context:         Segment C, LinkedIn, sent DS-1, replied warmly, now this
- What they want:  Wants a 15-min call to decide between Self-Study and Mentorship, asking if there's
                   any deal for paying now.
- Why escalating:  Serious lead + pricing beyond approved codes
- Suggested next:  Adam takes the call; she seems ready to reserve founding.
```

After posting the card, set CRM **Escalated? = Yes**, update **Status** and **Last touch**, and (if
appropriate) send the approved holding reply from `LEAD_REPLY_DECISION_TREE.md` so the person knows a
real answer is coming.

---

## 3. SLA — what Adam owns

- **Adam answers serious leads only.** Escalated serious leads (and true RED items: complaints,
  press, legal/PR, off-script pricing) are his to handle.
- You keep the conversation warm with an approved holding reply until he responds, but you do not
  make the call, quote a custom price, or commit him to anything.
- Adam reviews **one weekly report** — routine volume, reply rates, and signups roll up there, not
  into escalations. Do not escalate things that belong in the weekly report.

---

## 4. What NOT to escalate (handle these yourself)

These are routine — use the approved scripts and snippets, then log in the CRM. Sending them to Adam
defeats the "approve once, reuse forever" model.

- Curious / positive replies -> snippet GREEN-A (`LEAD_REPLY_DECISION_TREE.md`).
- Factual FAQ questions covered by snippet GREEN-B.
- Someone ready to start / wants the link -> snippet GREEN-C; log the signup.
- Mild objections / "not now" -> YELLOW snippet, log, and watch.
- Personalizing the name, opening line, or one detail of an approved template.
- Routine sends and the single allowed follow-up.
- Adding a row to the CRM, normal tracking, normal logging.

For a **new template type** or off-script *wording* (not a live lead), do not escalate — route it
through the **"Pending approval"** path in `MESSAGE_APPROVAL_WORKFLOW.md` instead. Escalation is for
live situations and risks; template approval is async and separate.

---

## 5. Principle

**Escalate rarely, but always for the red list.** If you are handling more than a small fraction of
replies by escalating, you are over-escalating — re-read the GREEN/YELLOW snippets. If you are unsure
whether something is RED, it is: escalate. Protecting Adam's time *and* never dropping a serious lead
are both the job.
