# Zakarian GMAT - Daily Assistant Checklist

The daily standard operating procedure for the person running growth for Zakarian
GMAT. This is a focused, repeatable, 30-45 minute routine. Do it once per weekday.
Weekends are lighter (see the weekend note at the bottom).

Zakarian GMAT is a premium GMAT Focus Edition prep platform, currently in private
beta and free during beta (full access, invite-based, no credit card). You are
helping a real founder run an honest, manual launch. Everything here is one-to-one
and personal. There is no automation, no scraping, and no bulk messaging.

**GMAT is a registered trademark of GMAC, which does not endorse and is not
affiliated with this product.**

## Companion files (read these, do not duplicate them)

- `OUTREACH_SCRIPTS.md` - the approved message templates you are allowed to send.
- `CRM_WORKFLOW.md` - how the "Outreach CRM" works: columns, statuses, how to log.
- `REFERRAL_TRACKING.md` - how the `/refer` link and the $50 reward work.
- `WEEKLY_SOP.md` - the weekly rhythm and the Friday report.
- `../CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` - the 30-day plan: what to post and who to target each day.
- `../LAUNCH_ASSETS.md` - the public post drafts, positioning, and comment-reply lines.
- The lead reply decision tree (the green / yellow / red triage rules) lives in
  `LEAD_REPLY_DECISION_TREE.md`. Use it every time you triage a reply.

## The big picture: what you can decide vs. what only Adam decides

You handle the day-to-day: outreach, replies, engagement, drafting content, logging.
You **never** invent prices, discounts, or discount codes; you **never** make
score promises; you **never** imply any affiliation with GMAC, mba.com, or the
official GMAT. Pricing and discounts are Adam-approved only. Serious leads get
escalated to Adam (see the decision tree). When in doubt, escalate, do not guess.

## Daily targets (small and realistic - quality over volume)

| Activity | Daily target |
|----------|--------------|
| New personalized outreaches sent | 4-6 |
| Replies triaged and handled | all of them, same day |
| Posts/comments engaged with genuinely (your own account) | 3-5 |
| Day's content drafted for Adam to approve | 1 |
| `/refer` link shared where natural | 1-2 |
| CRM rows updated | one per touch, no exceptions |

Do not exceed 6 outreaches in a day to hit a number. A weak, generic message is
worse than no message. **Prefer no send over a bad send.**

## MORNING BLOCK (about 25-30 minutes)

### 1. Check the CRM for follow-ups due today (5 min)

- Open the "Outreach CRM" (see `CRM_WORKFLOW.md`).
- Filter / sort by the **Next action + date** column for anything dated today or
  earlier (overdue).
- These are your first priority - warm threads you already started. Handle them
  before any cold outreach.
- For each one: do the next action (a follow-up message, a check-in, sending a
  link), then update **Last touch** and set a new **Next action + date**.

### 2. Check for new replies and triage them (8-10 min)

- Check every channel the assistant works from: your own LinkedIn account, your
  own email, and any community threads you are part of. Work only from your own
  accounts - never log into Adam's accounts and never use his credentials.
- For each new reply, run the **lead reply decision tree** in `LEAD_REPLY_DECISION_TREE.md`:
  - **Green (clearly positive / ready to act):** they want the beta, the study
    plan, a sample chapter, or to reserve founding access. Handle it now with the
    matching approved template. Send the website link. Log the move in the CRM.
  - **Yellow (curious but not committed / has a question):** answer helpfully
    using the approved scripts and the FAQ lines in `../LAUNCH_ASSETS.md`. Stay
    helpful-before-selling. One soft next step. Log it.
  - **Red (serious buying signal, sensitive question, pricing negotiation, a
    complaint, or anything you are unsure about):** do **not** answer the
    substance yourself. Escalate to Adam (mark **Escalated? = Yes**, set the
    Next action, and notify him per the agreed escalation route). Pricing,
    discounts, refunds, and score questions are always red.
- Update the CRM **Status** for every reply you touch (New -> Contacted ->
  Replied -> Interested, etc. - definitions in `CRM_WORKFLOW.md`).

### 3. Send a small batch of personalized outreaches (10 min)

- Pick **4-6 hand-picked targets** from today's segment in `../CONTENT_CALENDAR.md`.
  Segments: A) people who previously asked Adam for GMAT advice; B) warm friends /
  b-school peers; C) MBA/MiM applicants and admissions/consulting/finance contacts;
  D) club/community admins; E) early users (referrals/feedback).
- Use an **approved template** from `OUTREACH_SCRIPTS.md` as a starting point, then
  personalize the first line to something real and specific about that person. If
  it could be copy-pasted to anyone, rewrite it.
- Lead with the honest hook: a free personalized GMAT study plan plus full platform
  access during the private beta, plus free sample chapters, a free study-schedule
  generator, and a free error-log template. The baseline is an official mba.com
  practice exam the user takes themselves and enters in the app.
- Never call anything a "free diagnostic" and never promise a score. You may cite
  only Adam's own result (565 to 735, top 1%).
- Log each send immediately: Name, Segment, Channel, Source/UTM, Date contacted,
  Template used, Status = Contacted, Last touch, Next action + date (usually a
  follow-up 3-4 days out). See `CRM_WORKFLOW.md`.

### 4. Engage genuinely on a few relevant posts (5 min)

- From your **own** account, leave 3-5 thoughtful comments on relevant posts (GMAT
  prep, MBA/MiM applications, b-school life). Add real value - a tip, an honest
  reaction, a useful question. Do not pitch in the comment.
- You may borrow tone and angles from the comment-reply lines in
  `../LAUNCH_ASSETS.md`, but write in your own voice. No emojis.
- This builds a real presence so outreach does not look cold. It is not spam and
  not a numbers game.

### 5. Draft the day's content for Adam to approve (5 min)

- Find today's planned post in `../CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` and pull the matching draft
  from `../LAUNCH_ASSETS.md`.
- Lightly adapt it (freshness, a current hook) and send it to Adam for approval.
  Adam approves before anything is posted from his profile. If delegated posting is
  ever set up, the only compliant route is a LinkedIn Company Page with you added
  as a Page admin - never sharing personal-profile credentials.
- Where natural in the day's content or replies, include the `/refer` link
  (`https://www.zakariangmat.com/refer`). See `REFERRAL_TRACKING.md`.

## END-OF-DAY BLOCK (about 10 minutes)

### 6. Update the CRM for every touch (6 min)

- Every message you sent, every reply you handled, every escalation: make sure it
  has a row and the row is current.
- For each: correct **Status**, **Last touch** = today, a concrete **Next action
  + date**, **Escalated?** set if you handed it to Adam, **Referred by** filled if
  it came through a referral, **Owner** = you, and any useful **Notes**.
- Dedupe as you go - if you find the same person twice, merge per `CRM_WORKFLOW.md`.

### 7. Log the day's numbers (4 min)

Record a one-line daily tally (in the CRM's daily-log tab or wherever the team
keeps it). These roll up into the Friday report (`WEEKLY_SOP.md` /
`WEEKLY_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`):

- Outreaches sent today
- Replies received / handled
- Greens handled, reds escalated
- New Interested / Beta signups / Baseline set / Founding reserved
- `/refer` links shared
- Anything notable (a great reply, a recurring objection, a content idea)

## Each conversion moment (do it immediately, do not batch)

- **Someone wants in / reserves founding access:** point them to the founding
  reservation form on the homepage or `/pricing` (email capture, no charge now),
  confirm, and set Status to Beta signup or Founding reserved as appropriate. Log it.
- **Someone gives positive feedback:** thank them, ask permission to share it, and
  ask who they would refer. Share the `/refer` link.
- **Someone refers a friend:** record **Referred by** on the friend's row and log
  it in the referral ledger (`REFERRAL_TRACKING.md`).

## Hard rules (never break these)

- Manual and personal only. No automated DMs, no scraping, no mass copy-paste, no
  bought lists, no outreach bots.
- Work only from your own accounts and email. Never ask for, share, or use Adam's
  passwords. Never log into his personal accounts.
- No emojis anywhere.
- Never say "free diagnostic" and never claim a scored diagnostic or placement test
  exists - there is none.
- Never promise or imply a guaranteed score or specific improvement. Cite only
  Adam's 565 to 735 / top 1%.
- Never imply affiliation with GMAC, mba.com, or the official GMAT. Include the
  GMAC non-affiliation line in any public-facing claim.
- Never invent prices or discount codes. Pricing and discounts are Adam-approved
  only. Escalate all pricing questions.
- Help before selling. If a message has no value in it, do not send it.

## Weekend note

Weekends are lighter: triage and reply to anything that came in, do follow-ups
that are due, and engage on a couple of posts. No new cold outreach batch and no
new posts unless the calendar specifically calls for one. Keep the CRM current.
