# Weekly Performance Report

The one-page report the assistant fills in each week and Adam reviews in about 5 minutes. Reviewing this is one of Adam's 5 tasks — keep it short, factual, and skimmable. The assistant pulls every number from the "Outreach CRM" spreadsheet (`../CRM_TEMPLATE.csv` columns) and the product's funnel analytics, then writes the report.

Use this alongside `14_DAY_LAUNCH_EXPERIMENT.md` (the targets and the Day-14 gate), `DAILY_ASSISTANT_CHECKLIST.md` (the daily rhythm the numbers come from), and `../REFERRAL_AND_DISCOUNT_PLAN.md` (referral reward mechanics).

GMAT is a registered trademark of GMAC, which does not endorse and is not affiliated with this product.

How to use:
- Fill the **blank template** below each week. Keep it to one page.
- A short **filled-in example** follows, so the assistant knows the expected level of detail.
- Numbers split by **segment (A-E)** and **channel** wherever a split is asked for.
- Every serious lead and every blocker must be listed — this is how Adam stays inside his 5 tasks.
