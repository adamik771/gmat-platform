# Lead Reply Decision Tree

How to handle any reply you get. Replies are color-coded: **GREEN** (you handle it with approved
snippets), **YELLOW** (you handle it with an approved snippet, then log and watch), and **RED**
(escalate to Adam — these are the serious leads). When in doubt, treat it as RED and escalate.

Sibling docs:
- `OUTREACH_SCRIPTS.md` — the scripts you sent that generated these replies.
- `OUTREACH_QA_RULES.md` — what you can and cannot say in any reply.
- `MESSAGE_APPROVAL_WORKFLOW.md` — what you may personalize vs. lock.
- `ESCALATION_RULES.md` — the full escalation rules and lead-card format (cross-referenced below).

---

## The tree at a glance

```
Reply comes in
   |
   |-- Curious / positive ........................ GREEN -> snippet GREEN-A + link + offer setup help
   |-- Factual question (FAQ) .................... GREEN -> snippet GREEN-B (approved FAQ answers)
   |-- Ready to start / wants access ............. GREEN -> snippet GREEN-C + log signup
   |
   |-- Mild objection / "not now" / unsure ....... YELLOW -> answer with approved snippet, log, watch
   |
   |-- Wants a call / ready to pay / negotiating . RED -> ESCALATE (serious lead)
   |-- Complaint / upset / public criticism ...... RED -> ESCALATE
   |-- Press / media ............................. RED -> ESCALATE
   |-- Pricing/discount beyond approved codes .... RED -> ESCALATE
   |-- Anything off-script or you're unsure ...... RED -> ESCALATE
```

## GREEN — you handle it with approved snippets

These are routine. Handle them yourself with the snippets below, then log in the CRM. No escalation.

### GREEN: curious / positive

**Signals:** "Sounds interesting," "Tell me more," "How does it work?"

**Snippet GREEN-A:**

> Glad it caught your eye. Zakarian GMAT is a GMAT Focus prep platform built by Adam Zakarian, who
> went from 565 to 735 (top 1%) as a non-native speaker. Right now it's free during a
> private beta — full access, no card: 50+ chapters, 1,900+ practice questions, mock exams, and a
> personalized study plan.
>
> Here's the link: https://www.zakariangmat.com. If you want, I'll help you get a free personalized
> study plan set up — happy to walk you through it. GMAT is a registered trademark of GMAC, which
> does not endorse and is not affiliated with this product.

**Then:** log Status = **Interested**, note what they asked, set a next action.

### GREEN: factual FAQ

**Signals:** a specific factual question. Answer **only** from the approved snippets below. If the
question is not covered here, treat it as RED and escalate.

**Snippet GREEN-B (approved FAQ answers):**

- **"Is it really free?"** -> "Yes — it's free during the private beta. Full platform access, no
  credit card."
- **"What's in it?"** -> "50+ chapters across Quant, Verbal, and Data Insights, 1,900+ original
  practice questions, full-length mock exams, analytics, an adaptive study plan, and an error log
  with spaced review. There's an optional AI tutor too."
- **"Who made it?"** -> "Adam Zakarian — he scored 735 (top 1%) on the GMAT Focus as a
  non-native English speaker and built the platform solo from the system he used to go from 565 to
  735."
- **"Is there a diagnostic / placement test?"** -> "There's no scored diagnostic. You set your
  baseline by taking an official mba.com practice exam yourself and entering your scores in the app
  — then it builds your personalized study plan around your weak areas." (Never call anything a
  "free diagnostic.")
- **"Will it raise my score / guarantee a result?"** -> "I can't promise any specific score — no one
  honestly can. What I can tell you is it's the exact system Adam used to go from 565 to 735, and
  it's free to try during the beta." (Never promise or imply a guaranteed/specific score.)
- **"How much will it cost later?"** -> "It's free during beta. When paid plans launch, founding
  members lock in 30-40% off the standard price. If you want, you can reserve the founding rate now
  with no charge." (For anything beyond this — a custom price, a specific number they're pushing on,
  a code that isn't FOUNDING30/FOUNDING40 — escalate.)
- **"Is this affiliated with GMAC / the official GMAT?"** -> "No. GMAT is a registered trademark of
  GMAC, which does not endorse and is not affiliated with this product."

**Then:** log Status = **Replied** or **Interested**, note the question.

> Awesome. Here's the link to get started: https://www.zakariangmat.com — it's free during the beta,
> full access, no card. The key step is getting your baseline in (take an official mba.com practice

**Then:** log Status = **Beta signup** (or **Founding reserved** if they reserve), set "Next action"
to follow up on whether they entered a baseline.

## YELLOW — handle with an approved snippet, then log and watch

These are not serious leads, but they need a careful, approved reply and tracking.

**Signals:** "Maybe later," "I'm using something else," "Not sure I have time," a mild objection.

**How to handle:** reply with one warm, no-pressure approved line — do not push. Example:

> Totally understand — no pressure at all. It's free during the beta and the link will be here
> whenever the timing's better: https://www.zakariangmat.com. Good luck with the prep either way.

**Then:** log Status = **Replied**, note the objection, set a light "Next action + date" if a future
check-in makes sense (and respect one-follow-up-max). Do not invent incentives to overcome the
objection — if you feel tempted to offer a special price or deal to win them over, that's RED:
escalate instead.

## RED — escalate to Adam (the serious leads)

Do **not** answer these yourself beyond a brief holding reply. Escalate per `ESCALATION_RULES.md`.

A reply is RED if it is any of:

- **Wants a call**, **is ready to pay**, **is negotiating**, or **wants a custom price**.
- A **complaint**, an **upset** person, **public criticism**, or anything with **legal or PR risk**.
- **Press or media.**
- Anything about **pricing or discounts beyond the approved codes** (FOUNDING30 / FOUNDING40) — a
  custom number, a new code, a bulk/seat deal, a price they're pushing back on.
- Anything **not covered by the approved scripts/snippets**, or anywhere you are **unsure**.

### Definition of a "serious lead"

> A **serious lead** is someone who has moved past curiosity into commitment or a decision that only
> Adam can make: they want a call, they are ready to pay, they are negotiating price or terms, or
> they need a custom answer (custom pricing, a tailored plan, a partnership). These are the leads
> Adam personally answers. Hand them up promptly — do not let a serious lead sit.

### Holding reply while you escalate (optional, approved)

> Great question — let me get you the right answer on that. I'll follow up shortly.

Then escalate immediately using the lead-card format in `ESCALATION_RULES.md` and log Status =
**Interested** with **Escalated? = Yes**.

## Always, after any reply

- Update the CRM: Status, Last touch, Next action + date, Notes, and Escalated? if RED.
- Stay inside `OUTREACH_QA_RULES.md` — no banned claims in any reply.
- One follow-up max on non-replies.
- When unsure, treat it as RED and escalate (`ESCALATION_RULES.md`).
