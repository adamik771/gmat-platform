## 4. Anti-spam rules

- **No mass-identical messages.** Every message is personalized and relevant to the recipient.
- **No unsolicited bulk DMs.** No blasting. Reach people you have a genuine, true reason to contact.
- **No buying lists or followers.** Only contact people from legitimate, relationship-based sources.
- **Email must identify you and include a one-click opt-out** (e.g. "reply 'unsubscribe' and I won't
  email again"). This is built into the email script in `OUTREACH_SCRIPTS.md`.
- **Respect every platform's Terms of Service** and applicable anti-spam law.
- **One follow-up max** per non-reply. Never a second.
