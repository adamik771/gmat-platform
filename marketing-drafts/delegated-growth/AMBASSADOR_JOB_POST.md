# Student Ambassador — Brief and Recruitment Post

This file has two parts:

1. **The brief** — what the ambassador role is, who it is for, the rewards, and how
   to run it.
2. **The recruitment post** — a copy-paste message to invite candidates.

A Student Ambassador is a current GMAT-taker or business-school student with a real
network who genuinely uses Zakarian GMAT and shares it honestly with their own
circle. This is a perk- and reward-based role, **not** a paid employee. Ambassadors
work only within their own network and accounts. See `00_START_HERE.md` and
`ROLES_AND_RESPONSIBILITIES.md`.

---

## PART 1 — BRIEF

### Who it is for

- Current GMAT-takers, or MBA/MiM/b-school students, who have a **real** network of
  people studying for or about to study for the GMAT.
- Someone who actually uses (or will genuinely use) the platform during beta — not
  a marketer looking for a paycheck.
- People who are honest, low-key, and well-respected in their circle. Their
  recommendation works because it is real.

### What an ambassador does

- Shares their **personal `/refer` link** (https://www.zakariangmat.com/refer) with
  people in their own circles who would actually benefit.
- Posts an **honest personal recommendation** — only if they genuinely use and like
  the platform. No scripted testimonials, no exaggeration.
- Refers friends who are a real fit for GMAT prep.
- Optionally does a small, tasteful campus push (a study group, a class chat, a club
  channel they are part of) — where it is welcome and allowed, never spam.
- Gives **honest feedback** on the product so it gets better.

### Rewards (Adam approves exact rewards)

- **$50 per paying founding referral:** when a person they referred becomes a paying
  founding member, the ambassador earns $50. The referred friend gets the founding
  discount. (See `REFERRAL_TRACKING.md` for how this is verified and paid.)
- **Perks:** free or extended founding access to the platform, and public
  recognition (for example, a thank-you or credit) for top contributors.
- Exact reward amounts and perks are set and confirmed by Adam. Ambassadors never
  invent or promise rewards, prices, or discount codes.

### Expectations

- **Personal and ethical.** Real recommendations only. No fake testimonials, no
  invented results, no guaranteed-score claims, no implied GMAC/official-GMAT
  affiliation.
- **No spam.** No mass identical messages, no bulk unsolicited DMs, no buying
  followers. Every share is personal and relevant.
- **Own accounts only.** Ambassadors use their own profiles and network; they never
  ask for or use anyone else's login.
- **No emojis** in anything they post on behalf of the platform.
- Recommend it **only if they actually use it.** This is the most important rule.

### Time commitment

A few hours per week, flexible. This is meant to fit around studying, not replace it.

### How to apply

A short, honest note: who they are, what they are studying for, roughly how big and
relevant their network is, and why they would recommend the platform. There is no
pressure — if it is not a genuine fit, it is fine to pass.

### How many to recruit first

Start with **2–3** ambassadors. Choose quality over quantity: a few genuine,
well-networked people beat a crowd of casual sharers. Learn what works, then expand.

### Simple agreement (one paragraph)

> You will share Zakarian GMAT honestly with people in your own network who would
> genuinely benefit, using your personal `/refer` link and your own accounts. You
> will only recommend it if you actually use it, you will never spam or use anyone
> else's accounts, and you will follow the rules in `00_START_HERE.md` (no emojis,
> no guaranteed-score claims, no "diagnostic" claims, no implied GMAC affiliation).
> In return, you earn $50 for each friend who becomes a paying founding member, plus
> founding-access perks and recognition, with exact rewards confirmed by Adam. Either
> side can end this arrangement at any time.
