# Delegated Growth System — Start Here

This folder is the operating system a hired helper uses to grow Zakarian GMAT
without Adam having to run day-to-day outreach himself. It turns the existing
strategy documents (the marketing drafts in the parent folder) into a set of
roles, checklists, scripts, and rules that a non-expert can follow safely.

Read this file first. It explains who does what, the rules everyone must follow,
and where to find every other document.

Zakarian GMAT is a premium GMAT Focus Edition prep platform, currently in
**private beta** (invite-based) and **free during beta** (full access, no credit
card). The growth job is to invite the right people, help them genuinely, and
track everything in one spreadsheet — never to spam, never to over-promise.

---

## ADAM DOES ONLY THESE 5 THINGS

> Everything in this system is designed so that Adam's involvement is limited to
> these five items. If a task is not on this list, a helper handles it (and, when
> required, gets approval first).
>
> 1. **Approve the first message templates (once).**
> 2. **Provide the website link.**
> 3. **Approve discounts/pricing.**
> 4. **Answer serious leads only (handed up via escalation).**
> 5. **Review one weekly report.**

## The two roles

There are two kinds of helper. Read the comparison below, then read the matching
job post (`ASSISTANT_JOB_POST.md` or `AMBASSADOR_JOB_POST.md`).

| | **Growth Assistant** (paid VA) | **Student Ambassador** (perk / commission) |
|---|---|---|
| What they are | A hired virtual assistant | A current GMAT-taker or b-school student with a real network |
| Relationship | Contractor, paid by the hour or month | Volunteer / reward-based, not an employee |
| Pay | Hourly or monthly rate, optional approved performance bonus | $50 per paying founding referral, plus perks (free/extended founding access, recognition) |
| Main job | Run outreach, draft content, keep the CRM clean, prep the weekly report | Share the `/refer` link in their own circles, give honest feedback |
| Whose accounts | Their **own** account and email only | Their **own** network and accounts only |
| Volume | ~10–15 hours/week | A few hours/week |
| Reports to | Adam (weekly) | Adam (light check-ins) |
| Can publish as Adam? | No — drafts only, unless added as admin on a LinkedIn **Company Page** | No |
| Recruit how many first | 1 | 2–3 |

Both roles follow the same absolute rules (below) and the same CRM.

## Absolute rules (everyone, every day)

> Breaking any of these can damage Adam's reputation, violate platform terms, or
> break the law. There are no exceptions.
>
> - **Zero emojis** in any file or public-facing asset.
> - **Never** call anything a "free diagnostic" or claim a scored diagnostic /
>   placement test exists — there is none.
> - **Never** promise or imply a guaranteed score or specific improvement. The
>   only result anyone may cite is Adam's own verified **565 to 735 (100th
>   percentile)**.
> - **Never** imply affiliation with, endorsement by, or partnership with GMAC,
>   mba.com, or the official GMAT. Any public claim block must include:
>   "GMAT is a registered trademark of GMAC, which does not endorse and is not
>   affiliated with this product."
> - **No automating LinkedIn DMs. No scraping** LinkedIn or any site. No outreach
>   bots or automation tools.
> - **Never** ask for, share, or use Adam's LinkedIn password; never log into
>   Adam's personal accounts. Helpers work only from their **own** accounts and
>   email. The only compliant way to post on Adam's behalf is a LinkedIn
>   **Company Page** with the helper added as a Page admin — never personal-profile
>   credential sharing.
> - **No spam.** No mass identical messages, no unsolicited bulk DMs, no buying
>   lists or followers. Every message is personalized and relevant. Email must say
>   who you are and include a one-click opt-out. Respect every platform's Terms of
>   Service and anti-spam law.
> - Helpers **never invent prices or discount codes.** Pricing and discounts are
>   Adam-approved only.

Full rationale and message-level checks live in `OUTREACH_QA_RULES.md`.
