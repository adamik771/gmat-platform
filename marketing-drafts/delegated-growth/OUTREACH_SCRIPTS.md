# Outreach Scripts (Third-Party Voice)

Ready-to-use scripts for an **assistant or ambassador** who is doing outreach on Adam's behalf.

**You send these from your OWN account or email, as yourself.** You speak in the third person about
Adam and the product — you are someone who knows Adam / uses the platform / helps with it. **You never
impersonate Adam, never log into his accounts, and never send as him.** This is a hard rule (see
`OUTREACH_QA_RULES.md`).

> Adam's own **first-person** templates (where he writes as himself) live in
> `../OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md`. The scripts here are the **delegated, third-party versions** of that
> same outreach — reworded so they are true and natural coming from you, not from Adam.

Before sending anything, read `MESSAGE_APPROVAL_WORKFLOW.md` (what you may personalize vs. what is
locked) and `OUTREACH_QA_RULES.md` (what you can and cannot say). For replies, see
`LEAD_REPLY_DECISION_TREE.md`. To escalate, see `ESCALATION_RULES.md`.

---

## How to use these scripts

- `[brackets]` = fill in. Replace every bracket before sending.
- Each script has a **When to use**, the **Script**, and a **Note**.
- You MAY change: the recipient name, the opening line, and one specific detail (and your own
  sign-off). You MUST NOT change: claims, the offer, prices, codes, links, or the disclaimer. See
  `MESSAGE_APPROVAL_WORKFLOW.md` Section 3.
- Always send as **yourself**. Make the personalization real — if you cannot write a true, specific
  opening line for this person, do not send the message.
- Approved links only: `https://www.zakariangmat.com` and `https://www.zakariangmat.com/refer`.

The standard sender identity line (use your real details):

> *[Your name] — [your relationship to the product, e.g. "I help with community/outreach for
> Zakarian GMAT" or "I'm an early user of Zakarian GMAT"].*

## (a) LinkedIn connection note — from your own account [DS-1]

**When to use:** reaching a relevant 1st- or 2nd-degree contact (Segment C: MBA/MiM applicants,
admissions/consulting/finance) where you have a genuine, true reason to connect. Keep it short
(LinkedIn notes are length-limited).

**Script:**

> Hi [name] — I saw [specific true detail, e.g. "you're applying to MBA programs this cycle" /
> "your post about GMAT prep"]. I help with outreach for Zakarian GMAT, a prep platform built by
> Adam Zakarian, who scored a 735 (100th percentile) on the GMAT Focus as a non-native speaker. It's
> free during a private beta right now. Happy to share it if it's useful — either way, glad to
> connect.

**Note:** acknowledge reality — if you have not spoken before, do not fake familiarity. One specific,
true reference is what keeps this from being spam. Do not mass-send connection notes.

## (b) Warm referral — from an ambassador to a friend [DS-2]

**When to use:** you (an ambassador / early user) are personally introducing the platform to a friend
or peer who is actually prepping (Segment B or E). This is your warmest, highest-trust message.

**Script:**

> Hey [name] — I've been using Zakarian GMAT and thought of you since you're [prepping for the GMAT /
> retaking / targeting [school]]. It's a prep platform built by Adam Zakarian, who went from 565 to
> 735 (100th percentile) and turned his exact system into the app. It's free right now while it's in
> private beta — full access, no card.
>
> If you want, I'll send you the link and you can get a free personalized study plan set up. Want it?

**Note:** lead with the relationship and your real experience. Only send to people you would
genuinely recommend it to. If they say yes, use script (f) to send the link.

## (c) Personalized cold-ish email — with sender identity + one-line opt-out [DS-3]

**When to use:** emailing a relevant contact you have a legitimate reason to reach (Segment C) where
LinkedIn is not the right channel. Must include who you are and a one-click opt-out — this is
required by anti-spam rules (`OUTREACH_QA_RULES.md`).

**Subject:** A free GMAT resource that might be useful for [their goal]

**Script:**

> Hi [name],
>
> [One true, specific opening line — e.g. "We connected at [event]" / "[mutual] suggested I reach
> out since you're prepping for the GMAT."]
>
> I help with outreach for Zakarian GMAT, a GMAT Focus prep platform built by Adam Zakarian, who
> scored 735 (100th percentile) as a non-native English speaker and went from 565 to 735. It has
> 50+ chapters across Quant, Verbal, and Data Insights and 1,900+ original practice questions, and
> it's free to use during a private beta right now — full access, no credit card.
>
> If it's relevant, I'm happy to send you the link and help you get a free personalized study plan
> set up. No pressure at all.
>
> [Your name], [your relationship to the product]
> GMAT is a registered trademark of GMAC, which does not endorse and is not affiliated with this
> product.
>
> If you'd rather not hear from me, just reply "unsubscribe" and I won't email again.

**Note:** the identity line and the one-line opt-out are mandatory — do not remove them. One email,
one follow-up max (script (e)). Never bulk-send identical emails; personalize the opening line every
time.

## (d) Value-first community / group message — admin permission required [DS-4]

**When to use:** sharing in a club, cohort, or community (Segment D) **only after the admin has
explicitly said yes** to your message in script form. Never post a link in a group uninvited.

**Script (to post once the admin approves):**

> Hi everyone — sharing a free resource for anyone prepping for the GMAT Focus. Zakarian GMAT is a
> prep platform built by Adam Zakarian, who scored 735 (100th percentile) as a non-native speaker
> (565 to 735). It's free during a private beta right now — full platform access, plus a free
> personalized study plan and free sample chapters. Link: https://www.zakariangmat.com
>
> I'm [your name], happy to answer questions. GMAT is a registered trademark of GMAC, which does not
> endorse and is not affiliated with this product.

**Note:** get the admin's blessing first, in writing, and let them decide how/where it is shared. Lead
with the free value, not a pitch. One post — do not repeat it across the group's channels.

## (e) Follow-up after no reply — send ONCE only [DS-5]

**When to use:** ~4-6 days after (a), (b), or (c) with no response. This is the **only** follow-up.
Never send a second.

**Script:**

> Hey [name] — just floating this back up in case it got buried. No worries if it's not the right
> time. If it's ever useful, here's the link: https://www.zakariangmat.com — and good luck with the
> prep either way.

**Note:** light, easy out, no pressure. After this, stop — do not follow up again.

`LEAD_REPLY_DECISION_TREE.md`). Send the link and offer to help them set up the free study plan.

**Script:**

> Great — here's the link: https://www.zakariangmat.com. It's free during the private beta, full
> access, no card needed.

**Note:** the free study plan is the honest hook — lead with helping, not selling. Then log them in
the CRM (Status: Beta signup or Interested) per `LEAD_REPLY_DECISION_TREE.md`. If they ask about
price, codes, a call, or anything beyond the approved info, escalate (`ESCALATION_RULES.md`).

## Universal rules for every script

- Send as yourself; never as Adam; never from his accounts.
- Personalize the opening line truthfully; one specific real detail per message.
- No automation, no scraping, no bulk-identical sends, no bought lists.
- No guaranteed or specific score claims; no "free diagnostic"; no GMAC/mba.com affiliation. (Full
  list in `OUTREACH_QA_RULES.md`.)
- One follow-up max per non-reply.
- When unsure, do not send — escalate (`ESCALATION_RULES.md`).
- Log every send and reply in the Outreach CRM.
