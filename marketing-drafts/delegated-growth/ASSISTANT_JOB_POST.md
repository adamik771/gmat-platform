## PART 1 — HIRING BRIEF (for Adam)

### Role summary

A reliable, detail-oriented virtual assistant who helps grow Zakarian GMAT during
its private beta. The work is personalized, one-to-one outreach to a warm,
relevant audience; drafting LinkedIn posts and replies for Adam to approve; and
keeping the Outreach CRM accurate. This is a help-first, no-spam role. The person
must be a clear writer in English and comfortable following strict rules.

### Outcomes / KPIs

Measured weekly, reported in `WEEKLY_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`:

- **New qualified prospects added to CRM** (relevant + a real connection): target a
  steady, sustainable number, not a blast.
- **Personalized messages drafted and (after approval) sent.**
- **Reply rate** and **number of interested leads.**
- **Beta signups** and **founding reservations** generated.
- **CRM hygiene:** every contacted person has a correct status and a next action.
- **Zero rule violations** (no emojis, no banned claims, no spam, no account misuse).

Quality beats volume. A clean 30-message week with real replies is better than a
300-message blast.

### Day-to-day responsibilities

- Build a small, relevant prospect list each day from legitimate sources (see the
  segments in `OUTREACH_SCRIPTS.md`), respecting platform terms — **manual only**.
- Personalize approved outreach starting points to each prospect (no copy-paste
  blasts) and send from their **own** account/email after approval.
- Draft LinkedIn posts and comment replies for Adam to approve and publish (or
  publish on a Company Page if added as admin).
- Log everything in the Outreach CRM (`CRM_WORKFLOW.md`): who, segment, channel,
  template used, status, last touch, next action.
- Run the one polite follow-up where appropriate (never a third).
- Triage replies using `LEAD_REPLY_DECISION_TREE.md`; escalate serious leads to
  Adam per `ESCALATION_RULES.md`.
- Track referrals and the $50 reward status (`REFERRAL_TRACKING.md`).
- Prepare the weekly report for Adam.

### Must-have skills

- Excellent written English; warm, plain, professional tone (no hype).
- Highly organized; comfortable living in a spreadsheet.
- Follows rules and checklists exactly; asks before guessing.
- Reliable and responsive; available on a consistent weekly schedule.
- Honest and discreet (handling Adam's brand and warm contacts).

### Nice-to-have

- Familiarity with the GMAT, MBA/MiM admissions, or b-school culture.
- Experience with LinkedIn outreach done the ethical, manual way.
- Light experience with spreadsheets/CRMs and basic UTM links.

### Hours and engagement

- About **10–15 hours/week**, consistent schedule.
- Start with a **paid screening trial** (below), then a rolling monthly engagement
  with a 1–2 week notice period either way.
- Independent contractor; uses their own equipment and accounts.

### Pay guidance

Ranges are starting points; adjust to the candidate and market. Confirm the final
number with Adam before offering.

| Region / platform | Typical hourly | Rough monthly (~12 hrs/wk) |
|---|---|---|
| OnlineJobs.ph (Philippines) | $5–$10/hr | ~$260–$520 |
| Upwork / Contra (mixed global) | $8–$20/hr | ~$420–$1,040 |
| Eastern Europe / LatAm | $10–$18/hr | ~$520–$940 |
| North America / Western Europe | $18–$30/hr | ~$940–$1,560 |

**Optional performance bonus (Adam-approved only):** a small fixed amount per
verified **founding reservation** the assistant generates (for example, a set
dollar amount per reservation that survives a basic quality check). Keep it modest
and quality-gated so it never incentivizes spam. The assistant never sets or
promises any price or discount — that is Adam's call.

### Where to post

- **Upwork** and **Contra** — global freelance marketplaces.
- **OnlineJobs.ph** — strong for dedicated long-term VAs.
- **University job boards** — students who know the GMAT/MBA world.
- **MBA / GMAT groups and communities** — only where job posts are allowed; never
  spam a study group with a help-wanted ad.

### Paid screening trial task

Pay for this; it is real work and it filters fast. Give the candidate three sample
prospects (name, segment, one real detail each) and ask them to:

1. **Personalize the 3 provided outreach messages** to those 3 prospects using the
   approved starting points in `OUTREACH_SCRIPTS.md` (one per prospect).
2. **Log all 3 in the CRM format** (`CRM_WORKFLOW.md`) with correct segment,
   channel, template used, status, and a next action with a date.
3. **Draft one LinkedIn post** for Adam to approve, leading with the honest hook.

Score on: personalization quality, tone, rule compliance (zero emojis, no banned
claims), and CRM accuracy.

### Red flags to reject

- Any emoji, hype, or fake urgency in the trial.
- Invents a price, a discount code, a "diagnostic," or a score guarantee.
- Suggests automation, bots, scraping, mass DMs, or buying lists/followers.
- Asks for Adam's login or proposes posting from Adam's personal profile.
- Copy-pastes the same message to all three prospects.
- Implies GMAC/mba.com affiliation, or omits the trademark line on a public claim.
- Sloppy English, missed instructions, or no questions when something was unclear.

### Onboarding steps (once hired)

1. Share `00_START_HERE.md`; have them read it plus `ROLES_AND_RESPONSIBILITIES.md`
   and `OUTREACH_QA_RULES.md`.
2. Give them edit access to the Outreach CRM (`CRM_WORKFLOW.md`).
3. Walk through `MESSAGE_APPROVAL_WORKFLOW.md`; approve their first templates once.
4. Provide the website link and confirm the honest hook.
5. If delegated posting is wanted, create a LinkedIn **Company Page** and add them
   as a Page admin (never share personal credentials).
6. Set the weekly report day and the escalation channel.
7. Start with the `14_DAY_LAUNCH_EXPERIMENT.md` so they ramp on a small, measured plan.

**Title:** Part-time Growth Assistant for an education startup (ethical, no-spam outreach)

**About the role**

I run a GMAT prep platform currently in private, invite-only beta (free to users
during beta). I am looking for a reliable, well-organized assistant to help with
help-first, one-to-one outreach and content drafting — about 10–15 hours per week.

This is **not** a mass-DM or growth-hacking job. Everything is personalized,
manual, and rule-bound. If you like spreadsheets, clear writing, and doing things
the right way, you will do well here.

**What you will do**

- Build small, relevant prospect lists from legitimate sources (manual only).
- Personalize approved outreach messages to each person (no copy-paste blasts) and
  send from your own account/email after approval.
- Draft LinkedIn posts and replies for me to approve.
- Keep our outreach spreadsheet (CRM) accurate and up to date.
- Triage replies and flag serious leads to me.
- Prepare a short weekly report.

**You are a great fit if you**

- Write clear, warm, professional English (no hype).
- Are extremely organized and comfortable in spreadsheets.
- Follow rules and checklists exactly and ask before guessing.
- Are reliable and available on a consistent weekly schedule.

**Nice to have:** familiarity with the GMAT, MBA/MiM admissions, or business
school. Not required.

**The rules of this role (please read — these are firm)**

- No emojis in any message or asset.
- No spam, no mass identical messages, no bots, no scraping, no buying lists.
- Every message personalized; email includes who you are and a one-click opt-out.
- You work only from your **own** accounts and email. I will never share my
  personal login, and you will never post from my personal profile. (If we want
  delegated posting, I add you as an admin on a company page.)
- No invented prices or discount codes; no guaranteed-score claims; no claim of any
  "diagnostic" or test; no implied affiliation with GMAC or the official GMAT.

**Hours and pay:** ~10–15 hrs/week. [Insert your rate per the brief.] We start with
a short **paid trial task**, then a rolling monthly engagement.

**To apply:** send a short note about your relevant experience and answer this:
"Here is a prospect — [name, one detail]. Write me a 3–5 sentence, no-emoji,

rules above will not be considered.
