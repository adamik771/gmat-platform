# 14-Day Delegated Launch Experiment

A single, time-boxed test of one question: can a trained assistant, supported by 2-3 ambassadors, produce real beta signups and founding reservations for Zakarian GMAT in 14 days while keeping Adam's involvement to a handful of small decisions?

This is the **delegated** version of the launch. It runs on top of the same assets a solo founder would use, but the day-to-day work is done by a hired assistant, not by Adam. Pair it with:

- `DAILY_ASSISTANT_CHECKLIST.md` — the assistant's daily operating rhythm (the delegated version of `../DAILY_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`).
- `../CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` — the 30-day what-to-post / who-to-DM plan this experiment draws from.
- `../OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md` — the message templates (Adam's first-person voice; the assistant adapts, never mass-pastes).
- `../REFERRAL_AND_DISCOUNT_PLAN.md` — referral reward and founding-price mechanics.
- `WEEKLY_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md` — the one-page report Adam reviews at the end of each week.
- The CRM (`../CRM_TEMPLATE.csv`, the spreadsheet named "Outreach CRM") — the single source of truth for every contact and status.

## 1. Hypothesis

**One trained assistant plus 2-3 ambassadors can generate 12-20 beta signups and 5-8 founding reservations within 14 days, while Adam spends under about 2 hours total.**

We will treat the hypothesis as **supported** if, by Day 14, we hit the lower end of those ranges (at least 12 beta signups and at least 5 founding reservations) without Adam exceeding the time cap. We will treat it as **not supported** if we fall below those floors or Adam's time blows past the cap to keep things moving.

These are deliberately modest. The goal of the first 14 days is to prove the *system* works when delegated, not to maximize raw volume. A working, low-effort system that produces a steady trickle is the win; a one-off spike that needed Adam's constant attention is not.

### What we are really testing

1. Can the assistant run the daily checklist unsupervised and keep the CRM clean?
2. Do warm, personalized messages convert to replies, signups, and founding reservations at a usable rate?
3. Do ambassadors add reach without adding spam or risk?
4. Can the whole thing run inside Adam's 5-task contract (see Section 8)?

## 4. Targets

Modest, concrete, and split so a checkpoint can catch problems early. Ranges are the working band; the **floor** is the number that must be met for the hypothesis to hold.

### Daily targets (assistant, each weekday)

| Metric | Daily target | Floor |
|---|---|---|
| Personalized DMs / emails sent | 4-6 | 4 |
| LinkedIn / channel posts | 1 | 1 |
| Replies handled same day | all | all |
| New CRM rows created / updated | every contact | every contact |

The assistant works **weekdays only** (10 working days across the 14). Weekends are light: reply to anything live, otherwise rest. This keeps the cadence sustainable and the messages personal.

### Weekly targets (ambassadors)

Each ambassador does **one** light action per week, from their own account:
- One personal post or story about trying the beta (their honest experience), with the approved link, OR
- 2-3 genuine one-to-one intros to people who would actually benefit.

Target: each ambassador drives **1-3 referral clicks** and **at least 1 warm intro** per week. No bulk messaging, no identical copy across recipients.

### 14-day outcome targets

| Metric | Target band | Floor (hypothesis holds) |
|---|---|---|
| Messages sent (total) | 40-60 | 40 |
| Reply rate | 25-40% | 20% |
| Beta signups | 12-20 | 12 |
| Baselines set (official mba.com score entered) | 6-12 | 6 |
| Founding reservations | 5-8 | 5 |
| Referrals (friend reached via an ambassador / referrer) | 4-8 | 3 |
| Paid | 0 (beta is free) | 0 |

Note on "Paid": the platform is **free during private beta**, so paid is expected to be 0 in these 14 days. The leading indicators that matter are signups, baselines set, and founding reservations. "Founding reserved" is the real conversion goal of this experiment.

## 5. Metrics to watch

Track every metric **by segment (A-E) and by channel** (LinkedIn DM, email, Company Page post, ambassador referral, other). The split is what tells Adam where to lean in next.

Core funnel, in order:
1. **Messages sent** — volume and personalization quality.
2. **Reply rate** — is the message landing? Low replies = wrong message.
3. **Beta signups** — did they actually start?
4. **Baselines set** — did they enter an official mba.com practice score? This is the activation moment.
5. **Founding reservations** — did they reserve the locked-in founding rate?
6. **Referrals** — did anyone refer a friend (and was it logged on both rows)?
7. **Paid** — expected 0 during beta; tracked for completeness.

The single ratio to watch early: **warm message -> reply -> beta signup -> founding reservation**. If replies are low, fix the message. If signups are low after replies, fix the offer framing or the landing page. If founding reservations are low after signups, fix the founding-access ask.

All numbers live in the CRM (per-row) and roll up into `WEEKLY_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`. Funnel events also fire automatically in the product (`founding_reserve`, `referral_click`, `signup`, etc., each carrying first-touch source), so the assistant can cross-check CRM tallies against analytics.

## 7. Checkpoints

### Daily checkpoint (assistant, 5 min, end of day)
- Did I hit the daily DM floor (4+) and post (1)?
- Are all of today's replies handled and all CRM rows updated with status + next action + date?
- Any serious lead to escalate to Adam? (See Section 8 — escalate, do not answer beyond your brief.)
- Any blocker that will stop tomorrow? Note it for the weekly report.

### Weekly checkpoint (Day 7 and Day 14)
- Roll up the funnel by segment and channel.
- Compare against the Section 4 targets. Are we on pace to the 14-day floor?
- What worked / what did not; pick the top post and the best segment.
- Update the pipeline snapshot (counts per CRM Status: New, Contacted, Replied, Interested, Beta signup, Baseline set, Founding reserved, Paid, Lost).
- Fill `WEEKLY_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md` and send it to Adam for his ~5-minute review.

## 8. Adam's time budget (hard cap)

**Cap: about 2 hours total across the full 14 days.** If the experiment cannot run inside this cap, that is itself a finding — record it and raise it at the Day-14 gate rather than quietly spending more of Adam's time.

Adam does only his 5 things:
1. **Approve the first-message templates** (once, before Day 1). ~20 min.
2. **Provide the website link** (already done; one line). ~0 min.
3. **Approve discounts / pricing** (confirm the founding figures and that no codes are quoted publicly). ~10 min.
4. **Answer serious leads only**, handed up via escalation by the assistant. Budget ~30-40 min across 14 days.
5. **Review one weekly report** (Day 7 and Day 14), about 5 min each. ~10-15 min total.

Everything else — sending, posting, replying to routine messages, CRM upkeep, ambassador coordination, reporting — is the assistant's job. The assistant escalates a lead only when it is genuinely serious (a real buying / coaching question, a partnership, a press / club-wide opportunity, or anything needing Adam's personal credibility). Routine questions are answered from the approved templates.

The assistant tracks Adam's actual minutes on the "Adam time spent this week" line of `WEEKLY_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md` so the cap is measured, not assumed.

## 9. Day-14 decision gate

At the end of Day 14, the assistant presents the final numbers in `WEEKLY_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md` plus a one-line recommendation. Adam picks one of three:

### SCALE
**If:** at least 12 beta signups AND at least 5 founding reservations AND Adam's time stayed under ~2 hours.
**Then:** the delegated system works. Extend the assistant's hours, add 1-2 more ambassadors, and widen to colder segments (C/D) with the same personalization rules. Consider turning on the dormant ad pixels only after organic conversion is proven.

### ITERATE
**If:** signups OR founding reservations landed between the floor and roughly half the floor (e.g. 6-11 signups, or 3-4 founding reservations), OR replies were healthy but conversion stalled at a specific funnel step.
**Then:** keep the assistant, keep the cap, but fix the one weakest step before scaling. Run a focused 7-14 day second round changing only that variable (the message, the offer framing, the landing page, or the founding ask).

### PAUSE
**If:** beta signups under 6 OR founding reservations under 3 by Day 14, OR Adam's time had to exceed the cap to keep things moving, OR any rule was broken (spam, scraping, account-sharing, banned claims).
**Then:** stop the delegated push. Re-examine whether the offer, the audience, or the delegation model is the problem before spending more. A rule violation is an automatic pause regardless of the numbers.

Record the decision, the numbers it was based on, and the single biggest lesson at the bottom of the Day-14 report.

## 10. Guardrails (apply to everyone, every day)

- No automated DMs, no scraping, no outreach bots, no bulk identical messages, no bought lists or followers.
- Never log into or use Adam's personal accounts or LinkedIn password. Assistant and ambassadors use their own accounts and email only.
- Never call anything a "free diagnostic"; never claim a scored diagnostic / placement test exists.
- Never promise or imply a guaranteed score or specific improvement. Only cite Adam's 565 to 735 / top 1%.
- Never imply affiliation with GMAC, mba.com, or the official GMAT. Include the GMAC no-affiliation line in any public-facing claim block.
- Every message personalized and relevant; email includes who you are and a one-click opt-out.
- Zero emojis in any asset.
- The assistant never invents prices or discount codes; pricing and discounts are Adam-approved only.
