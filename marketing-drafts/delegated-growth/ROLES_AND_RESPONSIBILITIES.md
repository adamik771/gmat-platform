# Roles and Responsibilities

Exactly who does what across the three people in this system: **Adam** (owner),
the **Growth Assistant** (paid VA), and the **Student Ambassador** (perk/commission).
Read this alongside `00_START_HERE.md`. The two job posts (`ASSISTANT_JOB_POST.md`,
`AMBASSADOR_JOB_POST.md`) describe each role; this file is the precise split of
duties and the hard boundaries.

---

## 1. RACI-style responsibility table

**Legend:** R = does the work (Responsible) · A = approves / accountable (Accountable)
· C = consulted (gives input) · I = informed (kept in the loop) · — = not involved.

| Activity | Adam | Growth Assistant | Student Ambassador |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Outreach** (find + message prospects) | A | R | R (own network only) |
| **Content drafting** (posts, replies, emails) | A | R | C |
| **Posting / publishing** | A / R | R only via Company Page admin; otherwise drafts only | R (own honest posts only) |
| **CRM upkeep** | I | R | C (reports their referrals) |
| **Referral tracking** | A | R | C (provides referral details) |
| **Lead replies** | A (serious leads) | R (routine replies + triage) | R (within their own conversations) |
| **Pricing / discounts** | A / R | — (never sets or promises) | — (never sets or promises) |
| **Reporting** | A | R (prepares weekly report) | I |
| **Escalation** | A (decides) | R (raises per rules) | R (raises to Adam or Assistant) |

Notes:

- On **posting**, Adam is "A / R" because by default Adam publishes content himself
  (the Assistant drafts it). The Assistant becomes "R" only if added as an admin on
  a LinkedIn **Company Page**. See section 3.
- On **pricing/discounts**, only Adam may set or change a price or a code. Helpers
  state the offer using Adam-approved language only and never invent numbers.

## 2. What each role NEVER does

**Adam never:**

- Shares his personal LinkedIn (or any personal account) password.
- Lets a helper log into his personal accounts.
- Outsources the five things only he can do: approving the first message templates;
  approving discounts/pricing; providing the website link; answering serious leads;
  reviewing the weekly report.

**Growth Assistant never:**

- Uses emojis, hype, or fake urgency.
- Invents a price, a discount code, a "diagnostic," or a guaranteed-score claim.
- Implies affiliation with GMAC or the official GMAT, or omits the trademark line
  on a public claim block.
- Automates DMs, runs bots, scrapes any site, sends mass identical messages, or
  buys lists/followers.
- Logs into or posts from Adam's personal profile, or asks for his login.
- Sends anything before the relevant templates are approved.
- Answers a serious lead as if they were Adam — those get escalated.

**Student Ambassador never:**

- Posts fake testimonials or recommends the platform without actually using it.
- Spams, sends bulk unsolicited DMs, or uses anyone else's accounts.
- Invents rewards, prices, or discount codes.
- Uses emojis, guaranteed-score claims, "diagnostic" claims, or implied GMAC
  affiliation in anything they post for the platform.

## 3. Channel rules

These keep everyone compliant with platform terms and protect Adam's reputation.

- **The Growth Assistant works from their OWN account and email.** They may
  **draft** LinkedIn posts and comments for **Adam to publish**. Adam cannot and
  will not share his personal LinkedIn login.
- **If delegated posting is genuinely wanted, the only compliant route is a LinkedIn
  Company Page** with the Assistant added as a **Page admin**. This lets the
  Assistant post as the brand page without ever touching Adam's personal credentials.
  Personal-profile credential sharing is never allowed.
- **The Student Ambassador works only within their own network and accounts** — their
  own profile, their own DMs, their own circle. They share their personal `/refer`
  link and their own honest recommendation.
- **All outreach is manual and personalized.** No automation, no scraping, no mass
  identical messages, on any channel.
- **Email** specifically must identify who is writing and include a one-click
  opt-out, per anti-spam law.
