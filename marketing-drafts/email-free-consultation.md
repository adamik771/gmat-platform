## Panel notes (why the copy is the way it is)

- The story-led "i was stuck at 565 too" variant beat the reciprocity-led and zero-risk variants with every judge: the subject pre-qualifies exactly the plateau-feeling reader most likely to reply, and the vulnerability line ("busy, not improving, too stubborn to admit it") was rated the most authentic writing in the set.
- Grafted from the reciprocity variant: the "no pitch waiting for you at minute 25" trust line and the mine/competitor's/none triad.
- Dropped entirely: the zero-risk variant's deliverables list ("a concrete 8-week structure, an answer to the question keeping you stuck") — judged as over-promising for an ask-anything call and drifting toward diagnostic/landing-page territory.
- Deliverability: subjects deliberately avoid leading with "free" (spam-weighted token, weak open-driver on a warm list); the body carries the one required "completely free." No "for anyone on this list" bulk-mail tells.
