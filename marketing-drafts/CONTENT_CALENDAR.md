# Zakarian GMAT - 30-Day Launch Content Calendar

## Intro

This is a 30-day launch plan for Zakarian GMAT (zakariangmat.com), run by a solo founder who is also finishing a degree. The goal of the 30 days is to move a warm circle and adjacent b-school/MBA communities through the funnel: build credibility, recruit beta testers, activate them on the platform, open founding access, and start a referral loop.

Daily time budget is realistic: about 30-45 minutes per day. That covers one piece of LinkedIn activity (a post or comment engagement), a small set of hand-picked manual DMs, and follow-ups. Weekends are deliberately lighter (engage and follow-up only, no new posts). Nothing here is automated. Every DM is personalized and sent by hand; daily DM counts are intentionally small so each message can be tailored.

Post drafts live in the companion file **LAUNCH_ASSETS.md** (same folder). This calendar references post categories by name (for example "Founder/story post #2") rather than rewriting them.

### The honest hook

Lead with a free personalized GMAT study plan plus free full platform access during the invite-based beta (full access, no card). Supporting free items: free sample chapters, a free study-schedule generator, and a free error-log template. There is no in-app diagnostic, so never describe anything as a "free diagnostic" - the baseline is an official mba.com practice exam that the user takes on their own.

## Weekly Arc

- **Week 1 (Days 1-7): Warm circle + build credibility.** Reconnect with people who already trust Adam, share founder story and high-value GMAT advice, establish that this is real and free in beta.
- **Week 2 (Days 8-14): Widen + recruit beta testers.** Move into b-school and MBA-applicant communities, reach out to club/group admins, and start recruiting beta testers with the free plan + access hook.
- **Week 3 (Days 15-21): Activation + first feedback + open founding access.** Get signups through onboarding and a real baseline, collect first feedback, and begin introducing the founding-user offer.
- **Week 4 (Days 22-30): Founding-access push + referrals + double down.** Push founding-access reservations, turn happy early users into referrers ($50 each), and repeat the formats that worked best.

## Funnel Reference

1. **Awareness** - LinkedIn posts, warm DMs, student groups, b-school clubs, referrals.
2. **Interest** - free personalized study plan / error-log template / sample chapter.
3. **Activation** - signup, onboarding (target score + weak areas), official-exam baseline entered, plan shown.
4. **Conversion** - founding-user offer (reserve locked-in 30-40% discount, honored when paid plans turn on).
5. **Referral** - early users invite friends; friend gets founding discount, referrer earns $50.

### Audience segments for DM targeting

- **A** - People who previously asked Adam for GMAT advice.
- **B** - Warm friends/classmates at BI and other b-schools.
- **C** - LinkedIn connections in MBA-applicant / admissions / consulting / finance circles.
- **D** - Club/group admins (b-school clubs, MBA-applicant communities).
- **E** - Early users (for referrals and feedback).

## Weekly Review Checkpoints

Run each at the end of the week (Day 7, 14, 21, and a final wrap on Day 30). Keep it to 10-15 minutes.

### What to measure each week
- Posts published vs. planned, and which post got the most reach, comments, and saves.
- DM reply rate (replies / DMs sent), by segment.
- Free items delivered (study plans, error-log templates, sample chapters, schedule generators).
- Signups, completed onboardings, and baselines entered.
- Founding reservations (weeks 3-4) and referrals initiated / converted (week 4).

### Questions to ask
- What worked: which post format and which segment produced the most replies and signups?
- Reply rate: is the DM reply rate holding up, or dropping as outreach widens?
- Top post: what made it land, and can it be repeated next week?
- Drop-off: where are people falling out - no reply, reply but no signup, signup but no onboarding, onboarding but no baseline, or baseline but no founding reservation?
- Adjust: based on the above, what one thing changes next week?

### Week-specific focus
- **End of Week 1:** Are warm contacts responding? Is the founder story credible and clear?
- **End of Week 2:** Are b-school communities and club admins engaging? Enough beta testers recruited?
- **End of Week 3:** Are signups completing onboarding and entering a real baseline? Is the founding offer landing?
- **End of Week 4 / Day 30:** Total founding reservations and referrals; which formats and segments to keep for month 2.

## Guardrails

- Personalize every message. Never paste the same DM twice without editing it for that specific person.
- Keep DM counts small so each one is genuinely tailored. Quality over volume.
- Put links in the first comment of a post, not in the post body.
- No emojis anywhere.
- No fabricated testimonials, no guaranteed-score claims, no fake urgency or fake deadlines.
- Never describe anything as a "free diagnostic" - the baseline is an official mba.com practice exam taken by the user.
- No automation, no scraping, no mass-DMs, no buying followers.
- No implied affiliation with GMAC, mba.com, or the official GMAT.
- If reply rate is very low for two days in a row, stop and rethink the message and the segment before sending more.
