## RLSA — Retargeting on Search (the cheaper first retargeting move)

RLSA = layer a remarketing list onto your existing Search campaigns. No Display
needed. It lets you bid differently for past visitors who search again.

- **List-size minimum:** a Search remarketing list needs **>= 1,000 active members**
  before RLSA can serve. During early beta you will NOT hit this for a while — note
  it and wait. Display Remarketing (>= 100) clears first.
- **Mode = "Observation" (Bid only), NOT "Targeting":** keep showing ads to everyone
  searching the keywords, but apply a **bid adjustment** (e.g. +15% to +25%) for
  members of the A1/A2 lists who search again. Do NOT use "Targeting" mode (that
  would restrict the campaign to only past visitors and choke already-tiny volume).
- **Keep match types tight:** EXACT and PHRASE only, no broad — same rule as the base
  Search account.
- **Suppress converters:** attach the "All Converters" exclusion list (signup OR
  founding_reserve OR purchase_completed) as a **negative** audience so you don't pay
  a premium to re-show ads to people already in.
- **Optimize toward micro-conversions** (signup / lead_captured / founding_reserve),
  since purchase volume is zero during the free beta.

### RLSA ad customization
RLSA uses your existing Search ads — no separate copy required. If you later want a
