All web research was accessed 2026-06-29 unless a separate publication date is noted in the Date column.

### Notes on source independence and caveats
- WordStream and LocaliQ publish the SAME underlying benchmark dataset under sister brands — cite ONE, not both, as "two sources." All credible education figures trace to this single study.
- Optmyzr figures are independent of Google but are vendor studies (medium quality); the 2023 study compares only broad vs exact (not phrase).
- The statistical-significance sample sizes (Growth Spree) rest on standard binomial math, so the numbers are trustworthy even though the source is an agency (medium).
- Law-firm and legal-services sources (Buchanan Ingersoll, Trademarkia) are directionally reliable on US trademark doctrine but are not a substitute for the official Google policy or for counsel.
- The Think with Google / SOASTA page redirected during research; its headline figures (~113% bounce 1s→7s, 53% abandon >3s) were corroborated across snippets, not read verbatim this run.
